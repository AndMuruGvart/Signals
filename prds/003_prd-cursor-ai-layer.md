# PRD 003: Cursor AI Layer

## Goal

Turn the repository into a predictable Cursor workspace with reusable guidance, safe automation, and discoverable workflows.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `.cursor/rules` contains stack and workflow guardrails.
- `.cursor/skills` contains at least three custom skills, including observability guidance.
- `.cursor/commands` contains at least three repeatable workflows.
- `.cursor/hooks.json` contains at least two hooks with concrete utility.
- Marketplace skill strategy is documented reproducibly.
